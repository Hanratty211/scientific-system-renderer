# Scientific System Truth Contract

Before modeling, establish a machine-readable truth model for the figure.

1. Separate logical architecture, physical setup, component detail, and temporal mechanism unless the deliverable explicitly combines them as distinct panels.
2. Give each physical connection a source component and port, destination component and port, medium, direction, and path behavior.
3. Permit turning, splitting, merging, focusing, resizing, conversion, switching, pumping, or actuation only at a declared component with that capability.
4. Classify each relationship as physical, field, logical, temporal, contact, containment, or motion envelope.
5. Never pass a physical path through an opaque body, mount, enclosure, or unrelated component. A path may terminate at a documented active face or port.
6. Attach active areas and ports to the correct housing face. Keep them smaller than their parent housing unless authoritative evidence says otherwise.
7. Attach every elevated component to a plausible support and reject interpenetration, visible gaps at attachment points, and unsupported mass.
8. Preserve scale hierarchy. A microstructure needs a parent device and a linked inset; it must not float beside the system at an implied common scale.
9. Distinguish simultaneous hardware from sequential states. One reused device must not become a stack of physical devices merely to show time multiplexing.
10. Treat references as evidence. Record assumptions and unknowns instead of silently inventing geometry or behavior.

Critical unresolved topology, interface, scale, or boundary behavior blocks detailed modeling.
