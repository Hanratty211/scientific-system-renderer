# Identity, Scale and Route Clarity

Use for source-based reconstruction and feedback such as "I cannot tell what
this is", "everything looks alike", "wrong proportions" or "confusing wires".
These failures require geometry and composition changes, not just a caption.

## Lock a recognizable assembly

Before modeling, record five source-visible invariants for the chosen view:

- overall silhouette and installed or operating state;
- assembly relationships that carry meaning (handheld, worn, stacked, bonded,
  nested or mechanically coupled);
- distinctive geometry of the main apparatus;
- relative dimensions with source, uncertainty and acceptable simplification;
- the features that identify each essential instrument without a label.

Model the common frame and attachment geometry first. Do not scatter attached
parts to achieve left-to-right flow. A connected graph is not an assembly.
Do not replace a linkage with a generic wireframe, or a compliant structure
with rigid blocks just because the component count is preserved.

Silhouette estimates from a photograph are visualization estimates, not
manufacturing dimensions. Do not derive exact relative scale from objects at
different depths. Unknown ratios require the uncertainty workflow. Expose a
small feature with a separate, explicitly magnified view, not silent enlargement
inside an otherwise common-scale setup.

## Preserve material and environment identity

Assign material by part class. Metallic contacts do not make the entire PCB,
package, insulation, elastomer or housing metallic. Use material overrides at
part level rather than a global silver material. Inspect the result under the
actual lighting; a nominally nonmetallic material may still read as polished
hardware.

A biological substrate needs recognizable contour and surface character. A
thick rectangular slab with a skin color is not sufficient. Keep a schematic
cut edge separate from the attached device and disclose the context as an
illustrative surface, not an observed placement.

Air gaps, sliding interfaces, spring ends and flexible layers must remain
visible at the delivery scale. Adjust the camera or provide a detail view;
do not enlarge a functional gap without an explicit exaggeration declaration.

## Give essential instruments distinct identities

Use reference-supported differences in enclosure proportions, screen-to-panel
ratio, controls, connector groups and mounting style. A single generic chassis
reused for a source, receiver, supply and controller destroys useful information.
When only function is evidenced, keep the instrument explicitly abstract or
research its actual model. Cosmetic differentiation is not factual verification.

Identify the main apparatus and the support equipment. Keep actual scale in the
main setup; use a linked close-up for a small main device. Avoid blank oversized
screens or peripherals that dominate without conveying information. Do not
invent measured traces to make screens recognizable.

## Separate actual routes from explanatory graphics

Record the meaning of every color, line style and arrow before drawing it.
Prefer actual jacket colors when visible; otherwise use one neutral treatment
for equivalent cables. Distinct colors require a real distinction and an
explanation in the requested annotation or companion review. Do not force all
media to the same treatment when that would hide a real distinction.

Physical paths are continuous and terminate at interfaces. Logical relations,
wireless communication, motion and fields are not extra cables. Keep them in
an optional vector overlay or an explicit separate view. A no-text request is
not permission for unexplained colored or dashed arrows in the 3D master.
For one wireless relation, use one visual convention; do not superpose an arrow,
dashed path and wavefront arcs to say the same thing.

Plan route corridors after assembly placement and the per-face port map. Give
connectors a short exit in their outward direction and account for cable
diameter and bend radius. Preserve intentional harnesses and photo-supported
bundles. Do not turn wires into long right-angle floor tracks or route every
device through a common arbitrary foreground lane.

Trace routes in the final camera image, one at a time. Flag unexplained branch
points, overlapping parallel routes, crossings that look like junctions,
routes across screens, floating connectors and obscured destinations. A world-
space collision check cannot certify projected clarity. When endpoints remain
unknown, stop the affected connection and record the gap; neither a plausible
cable nor a silent deletion may stand in for a verified connection.

## Feedback-driven acceptance

Maintain case-level records containing: user feedback verbatim, affected
invariant, evidence needed, corrective action, rebuilt artifacts, agent review
and user acceptance. Mark the rejected version rejected; retain it as history.

After a shared-generator failure, repair representative assembly/material/
mechanism cases first and show source-render comparisons before a full rerun.
This is a staged repair, not permission to drop remaining cases. Explicitly list
pending cases and unresolved scientific details. Do not award a visual pass by
testing that a manifest contains the desired words.

Before delivery ask separately:

1. Does the unlabeled image still identify the same kind of system?
2. Are assembly, scale and material relationships faithful to the available
   reference, and are nonliteral details disclosed?
3. Can each essential route be followed without an explanation repairing the
   drawing, and are gaps or omitted connections explicitly acknowledged?
4. Can the scientific feature be seen at normal figure size, with a detail view
   where necessary?

Document actual findings. User acceptance stays pending until the user accepts.
Record qualified feedback such as "usable with reservations" separately from
unconditional approval. Preserve the user's wording and its batch/case scope;
do not promote a visual preference into scientific, wiring or manufacturing
validation. A favorable judgment of an overview does not resolve its recorded
unknown ports, hidden channels or omitted branches.
